# Applied Python Portfolio

Python notebooks that explore business decisions in retail and supply chain.

## Retail Sales Anomaly Detection and Promotion Simulation

Analyzes synthetic daily sales across eight stores, compares thresholds for
flagging unusual sales days, and estimates incremental sales under an assumed
3% promotional uplift.

[View the retail sales notebook](./Retail_Sales_Anomaly_Detection_and_Promotion_Simulation.ipynb)

## Supplier Sourcing Optimization

Evaluates 1,771 allocations across four suppliers and identifies 52
Pareto-efficient options for balancing modeled inventory and transport CO₂ emissions.

[View the supplier sourcing notebook](./Supplier_Sourcing_Optimization_Inventory_vs_CO2.ipynb)
